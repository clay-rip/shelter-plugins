## Very professional FAQ

### WTF is "Clayent?"

An experimental Linux-focused Discord client built with Electron, inspired by
[Recar](https://recar.loxodrome.app/). Currently in the early stages, and may
not see the light of day.

### Why is it called that? That name sounds stupid.

It's a portmanteau of my online handle + "client" because I'll likely be the
only person using it.

### What's Recar?

Another Discord client for Linux that you should probably use instead. It's
maintained by me and [@hamhim](https://codeberg.org/hamhim/) at the moment, and
has way more features.

Check it out here: [https://recar.loxodrome.app/](https://recar.loxodrome.app/)

### _Another_ Electron wrapper for Discord???

Recar is a really nice experience on Linux (for me, at least) but it's a pain in
the ass to maintain—it relies on Vencord/Equicord patches, so it breaks with
every update. Relying on Shelter mostly solves that, but Recar is already too
intertwined with Vencord to turn back now.

Also, the code is a bit messy IMO.
